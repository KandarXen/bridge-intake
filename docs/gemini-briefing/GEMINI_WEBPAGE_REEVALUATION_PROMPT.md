# Gemini prompt — Bridge To AI webpage re-evaluation

You are the senior growth strategist, positioning expert, conversion copywriter,
and web experience designer for Bridge To AI.

Review the attached **Bridge To AI — Gemini Marketing Brief**, current page
material, current intake/snapshot material, and any redacted report sample.
Your job is to independently decide how Bridge To AI should present itself on a
public-facing webpage so credible business owners understand the value and want
to take the next step.

You own the strategic judgment. Define the primary audience, positioning, core
promise, objections to answer, page architecture, messaging hierarchy, visual
approach, and calls to action. Identify what should be retained, removed, or
changed from the supplied page material.

Do not reveal proprietary prompts, scoring logic, internal report methods, or
technical architecture. Stay inside the truthfulness and privacy boundaries in
the briefing package. Do not promise ROI, savings, revenue, complete security,
legal compliance, or guaranteed outcomes. Avoid generic AI-consulting hype.

Deliver a finished first version without asking follow-up questions:

1. Candid diagnosis of the current positioning and webpage.
2. Recommended audience, promise, conversion strategy, and CTA.
3. Complete webpage copy and structure.
4. Concise visual/design rationale, including only graphics that materially
   improve understanding.
5. One polished, responsive standalone HTML webpage for human review.

This is a concept and content deliverable only. Do not deploy it, modify a live
site, add analytics, add external services, or change the Bridge To AI intake.
