Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026. Version: 2026-07-25-v1.55.

Plain English summary: Bridge To AI uses your interview answers to prepare your private AI opportunity report and related service materials. Your raw answers are encrypted, stored securely, and not emailed by default. When AI processing is used, identifying details are anonymized where practical before analysis. For partner programs, the partner may receive anonymized aggregate insights, but not raw member interview records or individual answers.

1. Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to the Bridge To AI intake interview and related report-generation process. Bridge To AI controls how the intake process is designed, what information is collected, and how it is used to deliver the requested service.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide before and during the interview, including:

  • your name and email address;
  • business name, website, industry, business type, role, team size, and department information;
  • interview answers about business model, customers, sales, operations, systems, risks, and AI-readiness;
  • technical records needed to save, resume, process, secure, troubleshoot, and verify the interview.

We do not ask for passwords, banking credentials, payment card numbers, private government IDs, or highly sensitive personal information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • save and resume your intake session;
  • generate private AI opportunity reports, advisor notes, and implementation recommendations;
  • send report links, notices, or service-related follow-up;
  • record whether you asked Bridge To AI to follow up, maybe follow up later, or only send the free report;
  • identify practical AI opportunities and risks for your business;
  • maintain system reliability, security, data integrity, and troubleshooting records.

We do not sell your interview answers, publish your raw interview record, or use your raw interview answers for unrelated marketing.

3A. Partner Programs And Aggregate Reporting

Some intake links may be offered through a partner organization, such as an association, training provider, or member program. In those cases, Bridge To AI may share anonymized and aggregated insights with the partner to help with education, course planning, member-support strategy, and program improvement.

Partner aggregate reports may include trends such as completion rates, readiness patterns, common AI education needs, sector-level pain points, adoption barriers, and general opportunity themes. They do not include raw interview files, individual member answers, direct contact details, or named business records unless you separately agree.

4. Encryption And Secure Storage

Your intake answers are processed through secure server-side systems and stored in encrypted form. Bridge To AI uses application-level encryption before storing sensitive intake content in Supabase. Supabase also provides platform encryption for data at rest and in transit.

The browser is not intended to store your full raw interview. It stores only what is needed to identify or resume the secure session.

5. AI Processing And Anonymization

When your answers are processed by AI, Bridge To AI uses an internal privacy layer called Hermes to reduce unnecessary exposure of identifying details where practical.

Hermes may replace identifying information such as names, emails, phone numbers, and similar details with placeholders before AI analysis. The secure system keeps the re-identification map separately so reports can be restored for the correct client.

Anonymization reduces exposure, but it may not remove every possible business-identifying detail, especially if you provide highly specific or recognizable business information.

6. Human Access

Bridge To AI does not casually review raw interview records. A Bridge To AI team member may access the raw interview record only when needed to:

  • deliver the requested service;
  • prepare, verify, or improve client reports and recommendations;
  • recover a failed submission;
  • investigate a technical, security, or data integrity issue;
  • respond to a lawful request or required compliance obligation.

7. Service Providers We Use

Bridge To AI uses trusted third-party platforms to operate the intake system:

  • Vercel hosts the intake application and server-side API processing.
  • Supabase stores encrypted intake sessions, outputs, logs, and related records.
  • Resend sends notification emails and report-related email communication.
  • Selected AI providers, such as Anthropic/Claude and other approved AI services, process minimized or anonymized prompts to generate reports and recommendations.
  • GitHub stores and manages application source code. GitHub is not intended to store client interview records.
  • Hermes is Bridge To AI's internal privacy, logging, anonymization, validation, and workflow-control layer.

These providers may process data only as needed to provide their services to Bridge To AI, subject to their own terms, privacy policies, security practices, and data processing agreements.

8. Email And Report Delivery

By providing your email address, you allow Bridge To AI to send you report links, status notices, and service-related follow-up. Raw interview files are not emailed by default. Email is primarily used for notifications and secure delivery links.

9. Data Retention

Bridge To AI keeps intake records only as long as needed to provide the requested service, maintain business records, improve the system, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and support client follow-up. You may request deletion of your intake record by contacting Bridge To AI.

10. Your Choices And Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or limitation of certain processing. To make a privacy request, contact Bridge To AI at team@bridgetoai.ca.

11. Security Limits

No internet-based system can be guaranteed 100% secure. Bridge To AI uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encryption, access control, anonymization, secure server-side processing, minimized email exposure, and data integrity checks.

12. Changes To This Policy

Bridge To AI may update this policy as the service, technology, legal requirements, or provider stack changes. The version shown at the top of this page identifies the policy version used for consent records.

This policy is intended as a practical operating policy for the Bridge To AI intake system. It should be reviewed by legal counsel before being used as formal legal advice for regulated or enterprise engagements.
